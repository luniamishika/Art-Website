Mishika Lunia

Artist and ceramicist based in India. Clay is at the centre of her work: functional ware and sculpture. She has been making ceramics for six months.

She picked clay because it is physical. She likes to touch it — it grounds her and reconnects her with herself. She works in stoneware and earthenware from Bhoomi Pottery.

She is also an academic.
